Content Angles & Post Generator

Step 1: Generate angles. Step 2: Create the post.

1. Prompt for Angles

Generated Prompt
I will give you a blog post.  
From that blog post, generate **30 curiosity-based content angles** divided into these 5 categories:

1. **Value** – insights, lessons, reframes, or tips extracted from the post  
2. **Story** – narrative angles, micro-stories, emotional hooks  
3. **Hot Take** – bold opinions, contrarian views, perspective shifts  
4. **Question** – curiosity-driven questions that spark engagement  
5. **Win** – small or big victories derived from the blog post’s themes

**Rules for generation:**

- Each angle must use a **curiosity hook** (not clickbait).  
- Keep each angle **1–2 sentences max**.  
- Do NOT mention the product, sales, or anything promotional.  
- Angles should create **intrigue**, **open loops**, and **emotional pull**.  
- Angles must be **based directly on the content of the blog post** I provide.  
- Provide **6 angles per category** (total 30).  
- Format them clearly under category headings.

Now say:  
[Insert Blog Post]

2. Prompt for Post

Choose one
Generated Prompt
Using the angle below, write a complete, engaging Facebook post in the style I specify.

Angle:  
[PASTE ANGLE HERE]

Category:  
[value / story / hot take / question / win]

Write the post with these rules:

- Use the angle as the curiosity hook in the first line, or create a stronger version based on it.  
- Expand it into a short, engaging post with:  
  - a micro-story or context (2–4 sentences)  
  - a clear insight, realization, or lesson  
  - an optional question at the end to spark engagement  
- Keep the tone conversational, human, and relatable.  
- Do NOT mention any products, offers, or sales.  
- Do NOT sound hypey or clickbait-y — stay authentic and curiosity-driven.  
- Length: 4–8 sentences total (short enough to read, long enough to deliver value).  
- Make it feel like a personal post on my Facebook profile.

Now generate the final Facebook post.
